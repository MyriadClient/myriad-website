# Cookie policy

_Last updated: 25 November 2026_

**myriadclient.dev sets no cookies**, and has no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, so there's no cookie
banner to click through.

## What's kept in your browser

| Name | Where | What it's for | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `theme` | local storage, on myriadclient.dev | your light or dark choice for the docs | until you clear it |

It stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

## Cloudflare

Cloudflare delivers the site and protects it from attacks. If it ever has to check whether traffic is a bot, it may set
a strictly necessary security cookie (such as `__cf_bm` or `cf_clearance`). These are used only for that check, not to
track you. See [Cloudflare's cookie policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/cookie-policy/).

## Accounts, later

Optional accounts are planned. They'll need one cookie to keep you signed in: strictly necessary, set only once you
sign in, and described here before accounts open. See the [privacy policy](/docs/privacy).
