# Jobs

Some modules work at length: a Printer building a schematic, a kit builder going from chest to chest, a search flying
a route. What they're doing is a **job**, and Myriad shows every running job the same way, whichever addon it's from:
the step it's on, how far along it is, and, when it's waiting, why.

## The Jobs window

Open **Jobs** from the launcher (`#job` lists the running jobs too; pick one to open the window on it). Each job is
listed under the module that started it:

- its name, state (running, stuck, paused) and how long it has run;
- what it's doing now, e.g. "Placing · 142 left", or why it's waiting: "stuck: no item: ×12 cobblestone";
- a progress bar, when the job knows how far it has to go;
- the steps under it, as they run.

**Pause** stops a job where it is (a build stops placing) until you **Resume** it. **Cancel** ends it and lets go of
everything it held: turned looks, held slots, borrowed items. **Cancel all** ends every job. A job that ended stays
listed for a few seconds with how it ended.

Turning a module off cancels its jobs, and leaving the world cancels them all.

## The Jobs HUD element

Add **Jobs** to the HUD (launcher: "Add to HUD: Jobs") for a line per running job:

```
Printer  Printing · Placing · 142 left · stuck: no item: cobblestone
```

**Lines** sets how many jobs it lists; **Show Ended** keeps a finished job's line for a few seconds.

## Notices

A job that fails tells you, with the module's name, the job and why ("Module: job failed: reason"), unless the module
handles the failure itself. A job stuck for five seconds tells you once, in a notice that replaces itself rather than
piling up, and again only if it gets going and then gets stuck again. Being out of reach doesn't count: moving puts
that right, and a Printer waits on it whenever you're away from what's left to build.

## Commands

| Command | Does |
|---|---|
| `.jobs` | lists the running jobs, each with its id |
| `.job pause <id\|module>` | pauses a job, or every job of a module (`.job pause printer`) |
| `.job resume <id\|module>` | resumes it |
| `.job cancel <id\|module\|all>` | cancels it, a module's jobs, or every job |
