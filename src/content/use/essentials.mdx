# Essentials

[Myriad Essentials](https://github.com/MyriadClient/myriad-essentials) is the stock set of modules. It's an addon like
any other, made for 2b2t: everything passes Grim by default. Each module's settings explain its options.

| Category | Modules |
|---|---|
| Combat | **Offhand** (a totem, crystal, golden apple or shield, falling back to a totem when it matters), **Auto Armor**, **Auto Disconnect**, **Kill Aura** |
| Movement | **Elytra Fly** (Recast for highways, Altitude for open country, Control where elytras are checked loosely), **Elytra Swap** (keeps your elytra from wearing out), **Lock Rotation** (keep flying the way you faced), **Rocket Boost**, **Auto Walk**, **Inventory Move**, **Velocity** |
| Player | **Auto Eat**, **Auto Mend** (mends your Mending gear with bottles o' enchanting), **Auto Sort** (sorts containers as you open them, showing the result at once), **Auto Tool**, **Inventory Tweaks** (opens shulker boxes from your inventory; drags, the wheel, and middle-click to sort), **Middle Click**, **Packet Mine**, **Reach**, **Stack Replenish**, **Wall Interact** |
| Render | **ESP**, **Blocks**, **Block Highlight**, **Storage**, **Tracers**, **Nametags**, **Tooltips**, **Full Bright**, **Time of Day**, **Free Look**, **Freecam**, **View Model**, **Zoom** |
| World | **Air Place**, **Portal Builder** (a lit Nether portal where you click, or on a tap bind), **Printer** (builds Litematica schematics around you, walking on and restocking from chests if you want; needs Litematica), **Scaffold** |

ESP, Blocks, Block Highlight, Storage and View Model show a preview at the top of their settings: a zombie, a block,
a chest or your hands, with the module's look on it while it's on, shader outlines and glow included. Drag it to turn
it, right-drag to move it, scroll to zoom, double-click to put it back. They draw on a [Preview window](/docs/use/menu#windows)
the same way, so one Preview shows everything that's on.

It also has two ready-made [control panels](/docs/use/control-panels): **Highway** (Recast, Elytra Swap, Auto Eat)
and **Cruise** (Altitude, Elytra Swap, Lock Rotation, Auto Eat).

Elytra Fly, Elytra Swap and Lock Rotation are separate so you can combine them: Elytra Swap works for any flight
(and for flying yourself, with *When You Fly*), and Lock Rotation holds the heading for Altitude and Control.
Altitude and Control are Myriad's own flights (other addons fly them too, such as Stash Hunter's Search), so they
keep the elytra, swap worn ones and come down to land the same way wherever they're used.
