# Essentials

[Myriad Essentials](https://github.com/MyriadClient/myriad-essentials) is the stock set of modules. It's an addon like
any other, made for 2b2t: everything passes Grim by default. Each module's settings explain its options.

| Window (and tag) | Modules |
|---|---|
| Combat | **Kill Aura**, **Offhand** (a totem, crystal, golden apple or shield, falling back to a totem when it matters), **Auto Armor**, **Velocity**, **Auto Disconnect** |
| Movement | **Elytra Fly** (Recast for highways and uneven ground, Altitude for open country, Control where elytras are checked loosely), **Elytra Tweaks** (keeps your elytra from wearing out, brakes before a wall you'd hit hard, starts the glide on a bind), **Rotation Lock** (keep flying the way you faced), **Rocket Boost**, **Inventory Move**, **No Slow** (full speed while eating or blocking, and through cobwebs) |
| World | **Scaffold**, **Placement Tweaks** (each on a bind you hold: place a block facing, or offset towards, the edge you aim at, in mid-air, or against a chest without sneaking), **Packet Mine**, **Reach**, **Wall Interact**, **Baritone** (Baritone's settings in the menu, and Custom Render: its path, work and goal drawn your way; only with Baritone installed) |
| Player | **Inventory Tweaks** (Mod + right-click a shulker box or ender chest in your inventory or a chest you have open to put it down beside you and give it a tab on the swap bar, opened at once from your inventory; they go back together, into the container they came from, when you close the screen. Remote Ender Chest keeps an ender chest open after you close it, from anywhere: your inventory key shows it again. Drags, the wheel, and middle-click to sort), **Replenish**, **Middle Click**, **Auto Reconnect** (joins again after a kick or a dropped connection) |
| Render | **Entity ESP**, **Tracers**, **Nametags** (an ender pearl's thrower is remembered, so a pearl in a stasis chamber is still named after they've gone or you rejoin), **Storage ESP**, **Block ESP**, **Block Highlight**, **Full Bright** (with ImmersiveVFX too), **Time of Day**, **View Model**, **Zoom**, **Free Look**, **Freecam** (each turns the other off), **Tooltips** |
| Automation | **Auto Eat**, **Auto Tool**, **Auto Walk**, **Anti AFK** (swings and taps sneak while you're away, so the server doesn't kick you for idling), **Auto Mend** (mends your Mending gear with bottles o' enchanting), **Auto Sort** (sorts containers as you open them, showing the result at once), **Auto Rename** (names the items you list at an anvil in reach, each stack back in its slot), **Auto Craft** (crafts the items you list as many as your inventory makes, their ingredients on the way, in your inventory or at a table in reach; with inputs and outputs, it puts what's made away and fetches the next round itself, shulker unloaders and loaders included), **Villager Roller** (rerolls a librarian until it sells the book you want, at the price and level you want, then trades it up if you ask), **Auto Trader** (trades with villagers from your input chests into your output chests, buying or selling, and keeps waiting for refills and restocks, so it can be left running), **Auto Farmer** (plants the crop you hold on every empty farmland in reach), **Printer** (builds Litematica schematics around you, sign text and water included, walking along the build and filling your inventory from chests or shulker boxes when something runs out, if you want; needs Litematica), **Auto Portal** (a lit Nether portal where you click, or on a tap bind), **Junk Dropper** (throws out the items you've picked as junk as soon as you pick them up) |

Entity ESP, Block ESP, Block Highlight, Storage ESP and View Model show a preview at the top of their settings: a zombie, a block,
a chest or your hands, with the module's look on it while it's on, shader outlines and glow included. Drag it to turn
it, right-drag to move it, scroll to zoom, double-click to put it back. They draw on a [Preview window](/docs/use/menu#windows)
the same way, so one Preview shows everything that's on.

**Inputs and outputs.** Auto Craft and Auto Trader keep lists of containers (chests, barrels, shulker boxes, a
shulker unloader's box): look at one and add it in the settings, or press **Input Bind** or **Output Bind** on it
(again to take it off); they're outlined while the module runs, and never shared in a config. From chat, a macro or a
wheel, `.containers <AutoCraft|AutoTrader> input|output` toggles the one you look at (`add`, `remove` or `clear` to say
which), and `.containers <module>` says how many there are.

Auto Craft, Auto Trader, Auto Rename, Scaffold and the Printer run as [jobs](/docs/use/jobs): they show in the Jobs
window, and what they open shows no screen, so they keep going while the menu, a wheel or chat is up.

It also has two ready-made [control panels](/docs/use/control-panels): **Highway** (Recast, Elytra Tweaks, Auto Eat)
and **Cruise** (Altitude, Elytra Tweaks, Rotation Lock, Auto Eat).

Elytra Fly, Elytra Tweaks and Rotation Lock are separate so you can combine them: Elytra Tweaks works for any flight
(flying yourself included), and Rotation Lock holds the heading for Altitude and Control.
Altitude and Control are Myriad's own flights (other addons fly them too, such as Stash Hunter's Search), so they
keep the elytra, swap worn ones and come down to land the same way wherever they're used.
