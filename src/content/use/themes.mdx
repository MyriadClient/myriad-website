# Themes

A **theme** is the whole look of the menu: colours, borders (with gradients, even rotating ones), rounding, gaps,
blur, shadows, how inactive windows dim, the animations and their speeds, the bar and the fonts. Open the **Theme**
window from the launcher.

## Presets

Myriad comes with **Myriad** (the logo's mint on near-black), **Tokyo Night**, **Gruvbox** and **Catppuccin Mocha**,
and addons can add more. Pick one and it applies at once.

Change anything in a preset and your changes are saved on top of it (it says *Edited*). **Reset** brings back the
original.

## Your own

**New** or **Duplicate** makes a theme you can name and give an author. Each is a file in `.minecraft/myriad/themes/`;
send the file to share it, or [share it as a code](/docs/use/sharing).

## Theme colours

The theme has a palette (accent, secondary, red, green, yellow, blue, magenta, cyan) that modules use for their
colours by default. So when you change the theme, Entity ESP, tracers, the module list and notifications follow. A colour you
set on a module yourself stays yours.

## Preferences

Some options are yours, not the theme's, and stay when you switch themes. They're under **Preferences** in the Theme
window:

- **Mod key**: <kbd>Alt</kbd>, Super or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>.
- **UI scale**, and whether the menu pauses singleplayer.
- **Title Screen**: Myriad's own title screen, or Minecraft's.
- **Myriad Cape**: whether you wear Myriad's cape (other Myriad players nearby see it; `.cape off` takes it off).
- Vim keys in windows, and more.
