# Sharing your config

Share your setup as a short code like `myriad_JS3k4d`. Anyone can look at it on the website, or take it into their
own Myriad in a click: as their settings, or as a control panel to try without losing theirs.

A share can hold any mix of:

- single modules, whole windows (an addon's category), or every module of an addon;
- control panels;
- your theme (its colours and style; not your personal preferences);
- keybinds, if you choose to include them (they're left out unless you do).

## Share

<Steps>
### Open the Share window

From the launcher: type `share`.

### Pick what goes in

Click a window's card to include all of it, or right-click to unfold it and pick single modules. Switch on control
panels, the theme, and keybinds if you want them.

### Share it

Give it a title and press **Share**. The code is copied for you. Send it, or its link:
`https://myriadclient.dev/config/myriad_JS3k4d`.
</Steps>

From chat: `.share module <module>`, `.share addon <addon>`, `.share panel <control panel>`, `.share theme`.

## What's never shared

Some settings hold things that are yours alone: a Discord webhook link, coordinates, a file on your computer, the
commands of a macro (which could include a password). Addons mark these as **private**, and a share never contains
their values. Others see that the setting exists, never what it's set to. Taking a share in leaves your own private
settings as they are.

## Look at one on the website

Open a share's link: the website shows its modules with their settings, drawn like the menu, in the sharer's colours.
Private settings show as hidden. Nothing to install to look.

## Take one in

In the Share window, paste a code (or a link) under **Take one in** and press **Look**. It says what's in it, and
whether it needs an addon you don't have. Then:

- **Try as control panel**: makes a control panel of its modules and settings and turns it on. Your own settings are
  untouched underneath: turn the panel off and you're back to yours. The safest way to try someone's setup.
- **Use these settings**: its settings become yours, for the modules in it (private settings excepted), and its
  modules are turned on or off as they were shared. Its control panels and theme are added too.

From chat: `.share try <code>` and `.share import <code>`.

Modules from addons you don't have are skipped, and Myriad lists them.

## Your shares

The bottom of the Share window lists what you've shared from this computer: copy a code again, or **Delete** a share
(it's gone from the website too). `.share` lists them in chat and `.share delete <code>` deletes one. Only the computer
that shared something can delete it.

<Callout type="info">
  Shares are public to anyone with the code. Don't put anything in a title you wouldn't post.
</Callout>
