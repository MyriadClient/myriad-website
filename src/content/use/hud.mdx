# The HUD

The HUD is what Myriad draws over the game: the watermark, the module list, coordinates, armour, totems and so on.
You arrange it on the **HUD workspace**: <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>, or the icon after the workspace numbers in the
bar.

- **Move** an element by dragging it. It snaps to the edges and the centre, and sticks to the nearest third of the
  screen, so it stays put at any window size.
- **Right-click** one for its settings: scale, colours, alignment.
- The **HUD** window there lists every element (from every addon). Click one to add or remove it; unfold it for its
  options. <kbd>Delete</kbd> removes the element under the mouse.

Essentials adds Watermark, Module List, Looking At, Coordinates, Armor, Binds, Chest Count, Direction, Effects, FPS,
HP, Ping, Player Count, Speed, Totems and TPS.
